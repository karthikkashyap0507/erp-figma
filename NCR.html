Description

Honeycombing over a 1.2 m length of the beam soffit. Bottom reinforcement is exposed over about 300 mm next to the beam-column junction.

Observations

Voids are up to 40 mm deep and there is no visible cracking. Rebound hammer readings near the defect average 24 against 34 on sound concrete in the same beam.

Probable cause

The beam-column junction is congested and was not vibrated enough. The batch slump was recorded at 75 mm against the 100–120 mm spec (batch ticket RMC-7781).
